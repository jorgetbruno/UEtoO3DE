## Coordinate conversion

- **Lane A (transforms)** — done in the UE exporter: cm → m (÷100), Z-up kept, handedness
  corrected in the rotation (exact quaternion component negation documented here once M1 lands).
  Invariant: every exported scale component is positive; never mirror a scale axis.
- **Lane B (geometry)** — UE's FBX exporter and O3DE's SceneAPI each apply their own axis/unit
  conversion. The correction contract is measured empirically in spike S0.2 and written down in
  [LANE_B.md](LANE_B.md). The `.assetinfo` sidecar generated by the importer must follow it.
